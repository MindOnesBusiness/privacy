03

Sharing of Information

The short version

We do not sell or rent personal information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

We may share data with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
  • Email and CRM tools (Kit, etc.)
  • Service providers used for analytics, hosting, or communication
  • Authorities if required by law

Who we actually use

For transparency, these are the third parties currently in the path when you use this site:

GitHub Pages
Website hostingReceives your IP address as part of serving the page, per GitHub’s own privacy statement.
Google Fonts
Typefaces used on this siteYour browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address in the process.
FormSubmit
Membership application deliveryReceives the details you enter in the application form and emails them to us. Its anti-spam check may use Google reCAPTCHA.

Each of these operates under its own privacy policy. If you would rather not be seen by any of them, you can email us directly instead of using the application form.

04

Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Analytics
  • Site functionality
  • Remembering preferences

You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

Where things stand today

This site is a plain static page. As currently built, it sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The section above describes what we may do, and covers the checkout, email-list, and other pages we link out to, which set their own cookies under their own policies.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of online transmission is 100% secure.

We also practice what we teach: we collect as little as we can get away with, because the safest record is the one that was never made.
